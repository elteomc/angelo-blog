# Workshop Quarto core

Scope approved in conversation: migrate the reviewed Workshop layouts into the real Quarto site, preserve existing URLs and Studio comparison, and verify locally without committing or publishing.

## Requirements

- Shared HTML layout and styles support home, indexes, series, articles and project pages.
- Quarto article sources remain authoritative for math and prose.
- Existing article URLs and other site pages continue to render.
- Content indexes derive from a small editable catalog rather than copied page lists.
- Light, Dark and System persist across navigation. Reading content works without JavaScript.
- Hide article subtitles, figure captions and series prerequisites while preserving source content.
- Studio remains in the isolated preview.

## Acceptance

Render all configured pages, validate internal targets and assets, inspect representative math and both themes at desktop and mobile sizes. No commit, push or deployment.

## Non-goals

New research content, a CMS, search infrastructure, or public deployment.

## Open questions

None blocking the approved migration.
