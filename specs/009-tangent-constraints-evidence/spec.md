## Evidence boundaries

Feasibility, optimal linear support, numerical evaluation, and nonlinear progress are different claims. A worst-case construction is not a frequency statement. Shared-setting point estimates do not establish tuned or seed-averaged gains. Probes sharing checkpoints do not count as independent trained networks. A matched predictive clock changes the experimental question and must not be presented as ordinary deployment performance.
